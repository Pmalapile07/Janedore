Before you get browsing…

A few useful things to know about shopping on Janedore.

Is Janedore a store or a marketplace?

It’s a marketplace: a place where independent South African brands bring their products together for you to discover and shop. Each brand keeps its own identity, products and business.

What kind of things will I find here?

Expect more than one kind of find. Browse clothing, accessories, homeware, beauty, scent, kids’ products and more as new brands join.

Who am I buying from?

You’re shopping products listed by the individual brands on Janedore. The platform brings them together, while each brand remains its own business and manages its stock.

Where will my order be sent from?

Brands fulfil orders from their own locations. The delivery methods available for your order will be shown at checkout.

Can I put products from different brands in one order?

Shopping across brands may depend on the items and delivery arrangements available at checkout. We’ll make the available options clear before you place your order.

How do I know which brands are on Janedore?

You’ll be able to browse the brands and their collections on the platform. We’ll also introduce new arrivals and labels through our updates.

Why should I join the early-access list?

You’ll hear when Janedore is ready to open and get the chance to explore the platform before it opens to everyone.

I have an independent brand. Can I apply?

Yes. We’re interested in hearing from independent South African brands. Use the brand interest form to introduce your business and tell us what you make.
