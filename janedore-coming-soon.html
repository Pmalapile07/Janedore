What is JANEDORE?

JANEDORE is a platform bringing independent South African brands together across clothing, accessories, homeware and more. It gives customers one place to discover and shop from different labels.

When does JANEDORE open?

We are preparing for launch. Members of our list will be the first to hear when JANEDORE is ready to open.

What does early access include?

Early access before the platform opens to everyone, plus updates on the brands and products joining JANEDORE.

Which brands will be available?

A considered edit of independent labels, from emerging designers to established names. Each brand will be introduced as we approach launch.

How can my brand be considered?

We welcome enquiries from independent South African brands. Register your interest and tell us a little about your brand.
