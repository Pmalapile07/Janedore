Good questions

A few things you might be wondering.

What is Janedore?

Janedore brings independent South African brands together in one place. You can browse clothing, accessories, homeware, beauty, scent, kids’ products and more, from businesses you may not have heard of yet.

Why shop on Janedore?

Finding independent brands is one thing. Knowing which ones are worth buying from is another. Janedore brings together a considered selection of independent South African brands, with an emphasis on quality, thoughtful products and a shopping experience you can feel confident in. Every brand we bring on should meet the standard we want customers to expect from Janedore.

What does early access mean?

Early access before Janedore opens to everyone, plus updates on the brands and products joining us.

Who made Janedore?

Janedore was founded by Pearl Malapile, a South African entrepreneur and developer with a passion for building online businesses. After experiencing the world of independent retail firsthand, she set out to build something of her own: a destination that brings independent South African brands together and makes discovering them part of the experience. What began as an idea has grown into a platform built to give independent brands a place to be seen, discovered and shopped.

How can my brand join?
