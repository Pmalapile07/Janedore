Good questions

A few things you might be wondering.

What is Janedore?

JANEDORE is a curated retail destination bringing together independent South African brands, emerging designers and individual makers. Discover clothing, accessories, beauty, scent, kids’ products, homeware and original pieces from people creating something of their own.

Why shop on Janedore?

Finding independent work is one thing. Discovering it in a place where quality and presentation are considered is another. JANEDORE reviews sellers and products before they are accepted, bringing together a considered mix of brands, designers and makers while maintaining a consistent shopping experience.

What does early access mean?

Early access before JANEDORE opens to everyone, plus updates on the independent brands, designers, makers and products joining the platform.

Who made Janedore?

JANEDORE was founded by Pearl Malapile, a South African entrepreneur and developer. It is being built as a curated retail destination where independent brands, emerging designers and individual makers can reach new customers, and where shoppers can discover original products beyond the mainstream.

Who can apply to sell on JANEDORE?

Independent brands, designers, makers and artisans can apply. You do not need to operate an established brand, but the work and products submitted must meet JANEDORE’s quality, originality and presentation standards. Every application is reviewed before acceptance.

Whether you run an independent brand, design original products or make things by hand, tell us about your work. JANEDORE is considering applications from independent South African sellers.
